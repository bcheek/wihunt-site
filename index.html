For Wisconsin hunters

Know what you can hunt, and when legal light starts.

WiHunt combines Wisconsin's season dates, shooting hours and license rules with your location and the licenses you own.

Download on theApp Store Coming soon onGoogle Play

Free. No account, no ads, no tracking.

Shooting hours for where you stand

Legal light and the end of shooting hours from sunrise and sunset at your location, with a live countdown, a home screen widget and alerts.

Standard or migratory bird hours

Switch between deer and small game hours and waterfowl hours with one tap.

What you can hunt today

Add your licenses, stamps and tags, and see which open seasons you're legal for and what's missing for the rest.

Your zones, found for you

Your deer unit and duck, goose, grouse, rabbit, bear and turkey zones, looked up from your location.

The whole season at a glance

A timeline of every Wisconsin season, what opens in the next 30 days, and a wishlist that shows the fewest licenses you need.

Harvest log

Log harvests, see season totals, punch tags automatically and get reminders until you register with GameReg.

Always check the official regulations

WiHunt is a planning aid. Regulations change, so confirm with the Wisconsin DNR's hunting regulations before you hunt. You are responsible for following all hunting laws.
